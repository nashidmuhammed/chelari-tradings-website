Engineering Benchmark

Architectural grade specifications.

We partner with globally recognized manufacturing standardizations, ensuring every square foot and every brass component performs under harsh tropical climates.

Download Technical Sheet →
Glass Standards: AIS / Saint-Gobain Toughened & Laminated — 6mm to 12mm thickness, acoustic PVB interlayers, custom fluted/reeded patterns with edge-polished CNC grinding.
Plywood Certification: IS:710 Marine Grade BWP (Boiling Water Proof) — 100% Gurjan hardwood core, vacuum pressure impregnated with phenol formaldehyde synthetic resin.
Hardware Mechanics: Solid Forged Brass & SUS304 Stainless Steel — Tested for 500,000 opening cycles. PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition) multi-layer coating resistant to coastal Kerala corrosion.
Modular Kitchen Tech: German Blum & Hettich Motion Hardware — Synchronized feather-light drawer runners, 65kg load capacity, integrated soft-close dampers, lifetime warranty.
Appliance Engineering: Schott Ceran Glass & Baffleless Extractor — High-efficiency copper induction coils, 9-level slider touch controls, 1400m³/hr extraction with auto-clean thermal sensors.
Showroom Location: Chelari, Malappuram (Calicut Bypass, Kerala) — Direct access on NH 66 with full material sample displays, live kitchen demonstrations, and dedicated architect lounge.
